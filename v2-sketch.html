evening

cambridge, ma · mondays, october – december

i.before

until recently, i only spoke of tanám in the past tense. first there was pamangan, a pop up that ran for five years. then tanám, the food/art/social project it became. tanám is my core, my heart.

ii.in love again

three years on, i'm ready to tend to what was left. the wound and the repair are bound up in each other, and neither happens alone. they happen with people in the room, over food.

iii.the table

evening is a monday night dinner series, made by people who are in it for the love of the game. each night is led by someone for whom that food or story means something. think dinner party.

iv.house rules

joy and rage are equally welcome.

v.the mondays

the calendar is alive, so nights may shift or change. see you at the table,
